📌 專案摘要與雙軌治理定位 📌 Executive Summary & Dual-Track Governance Positioning

本儲存庫為一項企業管治示範專案，展示如何完全運用現有 Microsoft 365 標準授權（零額外 SaaS 採購成本），搭建兼具「保密舉報（Whistleblowing）」與「員工調解（Workplace Mediation）」的企業級內控平台。

This repository is an Enterprise Demo showcasing a Zero-License Dual-Track Governance Portal built natively on Microsoft 365 standard connectors—integrating Protected Whistleblowing and Confidential Workplace Mediation without third-party SaaS licensing costs.

透過 Microsoft Forms、Power Automate、SharePoint Online 獨立權限清單及 Teams Adaptive Cards，建立「前端雙入口分流、後端高層機密匯流」的治理閉環。舉報通道對齊 ISO 37002 與香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）；調解通道則融入香港《調解條例》（第 620 章）之自願、中立與「無損權利」保密原則，明確承諾調解紀錄不寫入 HR 人事檔案。

Using Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, SharePoint isolated lists, and Teams Adaptive Cards, this architecture implements a closed-loop dual-track intake with automatic conflict-of-interest escalation. Whistleblowing aligns with ISO 37002 and HK PDPO (Cap. 486), while mediation incorporates the HK Mediation Ordinance (Cap. 620)—ensuring mediation records never enter HR personnel files.

入口 A • 舉報通道Track A • Whistleblowing
匿名防報復舉報表單Anonymous Intake (Form-01)

關閉記錄姓名（Record Name = Off），內建防自我暴露提醒與利益衝突閘門。Identity logging disabled; embeds anti-self-identification prompts and COI gate.

入口 B • 調解通道Track B • Mediation
保密調解預約表單Mediation Booking (Form-02)

強制勾選《調解條例》第620章保密確認閘門，並篩查安全風險與 HR 迴避。Enforces Cap. 620 confidentiality gate, safety screening, and HR conflict check.

常規路由 • HR 專區Standard Route • HR List
一般舉報與常規調解Standard Cases & Caucus

未涉 HR 之個案分別寫入「一般舉報」及「員工調解預約」清單並通知 HR Head。Non-HR cases route to HR_Cases and Mediation_Cases with Teams card alerts.

核心防線 • 跨軌升級COI Escalation • Bypass
繞過 HR 直達最高管理層Zero-HR-Access Escalation

舉報或調解一旦勾選涉及 HR，自動寫入權限隔離之「高層機密舉報」直達老闆。Any case implicating HR routes exclusively to Boss_Only_Cases; HR is blocked.
