📌 專案摘要與雙軌治理定位 📌 Executive Summary & Dual-Track Governance Positioning

本儲存庫為一項企業管治示範專案，展示如何完全運用現有 Microsoft 365 標準授權（零額外 SaaS 採購成本），搭建兼具「保密舉報（Whistleblowing）」與「員工調解（Workplace Mediation）」的企業級內控平台。

This repository is an Enterprise Demo showcasing a Zero-License Dual-Track Governance Portal built natively on Microsoft 365 standard connectors—integrating Protected Whistleblowing and Confidential Workplace Mediation without third-party SaaS licensing costs.

傳統企業吹哨系統往往面臨兩大治理盲點：第一，舉報管道由 HR 部門單一管理，當投訴對象涉及 HR 主管時缺乏利益衝突迴避機制；第二，缺乏非對抗性的早期爭議疏導管道，導致一般溝通摩擦被迫升級為正式投訴。本架構透過 Microsoft Forms、Power Automate、SharePoint Online 獨立權限清單及 Teams Adaptive Cards，建立「前端雙入口分流、後端高層機密匯流」的治理閉環。

Conventional corporate reporting channels suffer from two structural governance flaws: single-point HR administration that fails when HR leadership itself is implicated, and the absence of a non-adversarial early conflict resolution channel. Using Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, SharePoint isolated lists, and Teams Adaptive Cards, this architecture implements a closed-loop dual-track intake with automatic conflict-of-interest escalation.

在合規基準上，舉報通道全面參照 ISO 37002（吹哨管理系統指引）與香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）；調解通道則融入香港《調解條例》（第 620 章）之自願、中立與「無損權利（Without Prejudice）」保密原則，明確承諾調解紀錄不寫入 HR 人事檔案。

The whistleblowing track aligns with ISO 37002 (Whistleblowing Management Systems) and Hong Kong PDPO (Cap. 486), while the mediation track incorporates the voluntary, neutral, and "without prejudice" confidentiality principles of the Hong Kong Mediation Ordinance (Cap. 620)—ensuring mediation records never enter employee HR personnel files.

🏛️ 雙軌分流與利益衝突自動隔離架構 🏛️ Dual-Track Routing & Conflict-of-Interest Isolation Architecture

入口 A • 舉報通道Track A • Whistleblowing
匿名防報復舉報表單Anonymous Intake (Form-01)

關閉記錄姓名（Record Name = Off），內建防自我暴露提醒與利益衝突閘門。Identity logging disabled; embeds anti-self-identification prompts and COI gate.

入口 B • 調解通道Track B • Mediation
保密調解預約表單Mediation Booking (Form-02)

強制勾選《調解條例》第620章保密確認閘門，並篩查安全風險與 HR 迴避。Enforces Cap. 620 confidentiality gate, safety screening, and HR conflict check.

常規路由 • HR 專區Standard Route • HR List
一般舉報與常規調解Standard Cases & Caucus

未涉 HR 之個案分別寫入「一般舉報」及「員工調解預約」清單並通知 HR Head。Non-HR cases route to HR_Cases and Mediation_Cases with Teams card alerts.

核心防線 • 跨軌升級COI Escalation • Bypass
繞過 HR 直達最高管理層Zero-HR-Access Escalation

舉報或調解一旦勾選涉及 HR，自動寫入權限隔離之「高層機密舉報」直達老闆。Any case implicating HR routes exclusively to Boss_Only_Cases; HR is blocked.

⚙️ 技術架構亮點與開源工程交付物 ⚙️ Technical Architecture Highlights & Open-Source Artifacts

1. 零額外授權 TCO 優勢Zero-License TCO Advantage

100% 採用 M365 標準連接器，無需購買第三方昂貴吹哨 SaaS，數據完全留存企業自家 Tenant。100% built on standard M365 connectors, eliminating third-party SaaS fees while preserving full data sovereignty.

2. 清單級權限停止繼承隔離Broken-Inheritance RBAC

透過 SharePoint 停止繼承權限，確保即使同一網站下的清單，HR 亦無法窺探高層機密表單。Breaks permission inheritance on SharePoint lists so HR cannot discover or query executive-only records.

3. 安全輸出與防日誌洩漏Secure Outputs Log Hardening

在 Power Automate 讀取表單動作啟用 Secure Outputs，防止管理員在執行歷史中檢視舉報明文。Enables Secure Outputs on form retrieval actions to redact sensitive payloads from flow run histories.

4. 調適型卡片即時行動審批Teams Adaptive Card Alerts

依風險等級自動推送紅、藍、綠三色 Teams 互動卡片，一鍵直達對應隔離清單處理。Dispatches color-coded Teams Adaptive Cards with direct deep links to the authorized SharePoint list.
